I've spent the last few years writing *The Helix Moment*, a book on strategy and design in the AI age. After publishing it, I had a question: what if the frameworks in the book weren't just things you read, but tools your AI agent could actually apply?

So I packaged all 19 frameworks as installable skills for Claude Code and other AI agents. Things like:

- **Seven Faces of Strategic Dysfunction** — diagnostic tools that identify why strategy fails (paralysis, beautiful plans, hamster wheels, AI fog, etc.), each with a matched solution framework
- **5Ps of Loop Design** — a strategic design approach where humans and AI collaborate at every phase (Perceive, Perform, Portfolio, Pause/Promote, Progress)

I tested them by feeding real strategic scenarios to an LLM with and without the skills. The difference was striking — without the skill, generic advice; with it, specific diagnosis, matched solution pathway, and actionable next steps.
