# Helix Strategic Thinking Router

## Overview
You're standing at the intersection of strategy and design. Something needs fixing, building, or diagnosing — but which framework do you reach for?

This router maps your problem to the right Helix framework. Every skill in this collection comes from *The Helix Moment* by Suhit Anantula — a book about strategy as a living system, not a static deck.

**Core Principle:** The right framework depends on the rhythm of your problem — is it structured (▲ Lines), adaptive (● Loops), or emergent (〰 Vibes)?

**Book Source:** [The Helix Moment](https://suhitanantula.com/books/helix-moment)

## How It Works

The Helix Router operates on a simple principle: **match the rhythm of your problem to the rhythm of the right framework.**

1. **Identify the problem type** — Is it dysfunction, design, rhythm mismatch, AI integration, values, uncertainty, or strategy fundamentals?
2. **Match the environment rhythm** — Stable (▲ Lines), uncertain (● Loops), complex/emergent (〰 Vibes), or mixed
3. **Route to the specific skill** — Each framework is designed for a specific rhythm and problem type
4. **Apply and iterate** — No single framework is sufficient. Combine frameworks as the situation evolves

The router itself operates in 〰 Vibes mode — it's a sensing tool that helps you detect which rhythm your problem needs. Once you've routed to the right skill, that skill's rhythm takes over.

### Startup Navigating Multiple Challenges

A Series B startup used the router to navigate its strategic landscape:

- **Challenge 1:** "We have 3 competing product directions and can't decide" → Routed to `seven-faces-diagnostic` → Diagnosed as Face 1: Paralysis → Applied Playing to Win

## AI Integration

### Human + AI Framework Selection

AI can enhance the routing process:

1. **AI as Context Scanner** — Feed AI your organisation's strategic documents and ask: "Based on these documents, which Helix framework seems most relevant? What patterns of dysfunction, rhythm mismatch, or design need do you detect?"
2. **AI as Pattern Matcher** — Describe your situation to AI in natural language and ask it to match against the seven faces, 5Ps, and other frameworks. AI processes the pattern matching; humans make the final routing decision.
3. **AI as Framework Synthesiser** — Once you've routed to a framework, AI can help connect it to other relevant frameworks by identifying overlaps and complementary elements.

### Key Prompt

*"I'm facing [describe strategic challenge]. Based on the Helix strategic thinking framework, which skill should I start with? Consider whether my challenge is about dysfunction, design execution, rhythm mismatch, AI integration, values, or uncertainty."*

## Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Framework hopping** — Switching between frameworks without committing to one long enough to see results
- **Familiarity bias** — Always reaching for the framework you know best rather than the one the problem needs
- **Skipping diagnosis** — Jumping straight to a solution framework without confirming which problem you actually have
- **Single-framework thinking** — Believing one framework can solve everything. The Helix is designed to be combined.
- **Ignoring rhythm** — Applying a ▲ Lines framework to a 〰 Vibes problem (or vice versa). The rhythm must match the situation.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Problem type identified before selecting a framework
- [ ] Environment rhythm assessed (stable / uncertain / complex / mixed)
- [ ] Routed to the correct specific skill (not just browsing)
- [ ] At least one combination with another framework considered
- [ ] AI used to validate framework selection against organisational context
- [ ] Framework applied for long enough to generate real insight (not abandoned early)
- [ ] Results fed back into the router for re-diagnosis as conditions change

## Combining Frameworks

The Helix is designed to be combined — no single framework covers everything:

- **Strategic dysfunction + design:** Seven Faces → 5Ps of Loop Design
- **Uncertainty + AI:** Strategic Navigation → AI in the Loop
- **Values + execution:** EEE Layer → 5P Perform
- **Full transformation:** Helix Model → Seven Faces (diagnose) → 5Ps (design) → Co-Intelligent Organisation (scale)
