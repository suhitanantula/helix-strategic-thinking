# Seven Faces Diagnostic Router

## Overview

The Seven Faces of Strategic Dysfunction is a diagnostic framework from *The Helix Moment* (Chapters 08–15). Every organisation experiences strategic dysfunction — the question is which one, right now, is blocking your progress. This router helps you identify the right face so you can apply the right solution.

The framework uses the **LLV rhythm model** — Lines ▲, Loops ●, Vibes 〰 — to diagnose where your strategy is stuck. Each face represents a specific pattern of rhythm dysfunction, and each has a matching solution framework.

## Quick Diagnostic

### Step 1: What's the Primary Symptom?

Read these seven statements. Which one resonates most strongly with your organisation right now?

**A.** *"We don't know where to start."* — Too many options, no decisions being made, endless analysis without action
→ **Face 1: The Paralysis** (〰〰〰 scattered sensing)

**B.** *"Our plans never get used."* — Beautiful strategy documents that sit on shelves while teams do what they've always done
→ **Face 2: The Beautiful Plan** (▲▲▲ rigid structure)

**C.** *"Strategy feels disconnected from teams."* — Boardroom brilliance that never connects with frontline reality
→ **Face 3: The Ivory Tower** (▲ 〰 ● fragmented rhythms)

**D.** *"We're stuck reacting, not thinking long-term."* — Everyone's busy, fires are constant, but there's no shared horizon
→ **Face 4: The Hamster Wheel** (●●● reactive loops)

**E.** *"The world is changing too fast."* — Plans outdated before they're published, constant direction changes, teams disoriented
→ **Face 5: The Whiplash** (▲ vs 〰● static vs. dynamic)

**F.** *"We're unsure how to use AI in strategy."* — AI pilots multiply but don't connect, excitement without clarity, buzzwords without impact
→ **Face 6: The AI Fog** (〰●▲? disconnected capabilities)

**G.** *"We want innovation but keep playing it safe."* — Bold ideas get de-risked into nothing, innovation is theatre not courage
→ **Face 7: The Safety Dance** (▲▲▲ false security)

## When to Use This Diagnostic

**Use this diagnostic when:**

- Leadership senses strategic dysfunction but can't name it
- A new strategy cycle is starting and you want to avoid repeating past mistakes
- Teams are frustrated but can't articulate why
- You're bringing in a new strategy framework and want to ensure it addresses the right problem
- Post-mortem or retrospective on a failed strategic initiative
- Board or leadership team alignment on what's actually blocking progress
- You're coaching or consulting and need to quickly diagnose a client's strategic challenge

**Don't use this diagnostic when:**

- The organisation is in acute crisis mode (stabilise first, diagnose later)
- There's a clear, known execution problem (skip to the relevant face skill)
- You're looking for a planning template (this is a diagnostic, not a plan)

## Key Questions That Apply Across All Faces

These questions work regardless of which face you're experiencing. Use them to open any strategic discussion:

1. **What rhythm is your strategy operating in right now?** — Lines ▲ (planning), Loops ● (iterating), or Vibes 〰 (sensing)? Awareness of rhythm is the first step to changing it.

2. **What would have to be true for your strategy to work?** — Drucker's question. Surfaces hidden assumptions that may be driving dysfunction.

3. **Who needs to be in the room that isn't?** — Strategy fails when the wrong people are designing it. Co-creation isn't optional for complex challenges.

4. **What's the one thing you'd change if fear weren't a factor?** — Surfaces the courage gap. The answer often points directly to the relevant face.

5. **When was the last time your strategy surprised you?** — If the answer is "never," your strategy isn't sensing — it's just executing. That's a rhythm problem.

6. **What's the cost of doing nothing?** — Every face has an inaction cost. Making that cost visible creates urgency for change.

## How the Faces Relate

The seven faces aren't isolated — they form a landscape of strategic dysfunction:

- **Faces 1–2 (Paralysis ↔ Beautiful Plan):** Opposite extremes of the same spectrum — too little structure vs. too much. Both fail to connect thinking with doing.
- **Faces 3–4 (Ivory Tower ↔ Hamster Wheel):** Structural disconnects — one is vertical (levels don't connect), the other is temporal (present doesn't connect to future).
- **Faces 5–6 (Whiplash ↔ AI Fog):** Environmental mismatches — one is external volatility, the other is technological uncertainty. Both require faster learning loops.
- **Face 7 (Safety Dance):** The meta-condition — cultural risk aversion that can reinforce any of the other six faces. Often the deepest and hardest to shift.

**Most organisations have a primary face and a secondary face.** Diagnose the primary (most painful) first. The secondary often reveals itself as you work on the primary.

## Application Example

### Multi-Face Diagnosis: Southeast Asian Financial Services Firm

A mid-size bank ran the Quick Diagnostic with its 12-person leadership team. Results:

- **6 leaders** selected **D. The Hamster Wheel** — "We're stuck reacting, not thinking long-term"
- **4 leaders** selected **F. The AI Fog** — "We're unsure how to use AI in strategy"
- **2 leaders** selected **A. The Paralysis** — "We don't know where to start"

**Diagnosis:** Primary face = Hamster Wheel (●●●). Secondary face = AI Fog (〰●▲?). The paralysis was a symptom of the wheel — teams were too busy firefighting to make strategic AI choices.

**Intervention:** Applied Theory of the Business (Drucker) first to create strategic direction, then Lean Strategy + Co-Intelligence to integrate AI. Within 6 months, the bank had a clear 10-year vision and 5 AI experiments running with defined success metrics.

**Key insight:** When leadership disagrees on the face, the disagreement *is* the diagnostic data. Different organisational levels often experience different faces.

### Using the Diagnostic in Consulting

Strategy consultants use this router as a first-session tool: have each executive privately select their statement, then compare. The pattern of disagreement reveals organisational fragmentation (often Face 3: Ivory Tower) even when the surface symptoms point elsewhere.

## AI Integration

### Human + AI Diagnostic Partnership

AI can accelerate the diagnostic process without replacing human judgment:

1. **AI as Pattern Detector** — Feed AI your organisation's strategy documents, meeting notes, and internal communications. Ask: "Which of these seven patterns of dysfunction do you see most frequently?" AI processes volume; humans validate the insight.

2. **AI as Survey Analyser** — Run the Quick Diagnostic as an anonymous survey across the organisation. AI clusters responses by team, level, and function — revealing where different faces are operating simultaneously.

3. **AI as History Scanner** — Ask AI to analyse the last 3 years of strategic decisions: "What rhythm do our decisions follow? Are we mostly ▲ planning, ● iterating, or 〰 sensing? What does the pattern reveal?"

### Key Prompts

- *"Analyse our last 12 months of strategic decisions. Which rhythm pattern (Lines, Loops, Vibes) dominates? What does that suggest about our primary dysfunction?"*
- *"Our leadership team disagrees on which face we're experiencing. What questions should we ask to resolve the disagreement?"*
- *"Map our current strategic initiatives across the seven faces. Which face is getting the most investment? Which is being neglected?"*

## Pitfalls to Avoid

- **Forcing a single face** — Most organisations have a primary and secondary face. Don't oversimplify into one diagnosis
- **Diagnosing without the team** — Running this diagnostic solo in your head is less valuable than running it with the actual leadership team
- **Skipping to solutions** — The diagnostic is meant to route you to the right face skill. Don't try to solve the problem within this router
- **Treating faces as sequential** — Faces don't follow a fixed order. An organisation can experience Face 7 without ever experiencing Face 1
- **Ignoring the rhythm signature** — The LLV pattern is the most reliable diagnostic. The symptom statement is the entry point; the rhythm confirms it

## Combining with Other Frameworks

The Seven Faces Diagnostic is an entry point that routes to deeper frameworks:

- **Seven Faces → 5Ps of Loop Design:** After diagnosing the face, use the 5Ps to design the solution. Each face maps to specific Ps that need strengthening.
- **Seven Faces → Lines-Loops-Vibes:** The rhythm diagnosis from the faces connects directly to the LLV framework for deeper rhythm analysis.
- **Seven Faces → Strategic Navigation:** Use the uncertainty matrix to understand *why* the dysfunction emerged — often it's a mismatch between the environment's uncertainty level and the organisation's approach.
- **Seven Faces → EEE Layer:** Every face has ethical, emotional, and emergent dimensions. Use the EEE layer to ensure solutions don't create new problems.
- **Seven Faces → Co-Intelligence:** AI integration is embedded in every face solution. Use the co-intelligence framework to design the human-AI partnership model.
